About

Find each other after the trail ends

On long through-hikes, hikers earn a trail name — sometimes funny, sometimes mysterious, always a story. This directory helps hikers find each other after the trail ends.

  • Register your trail name with a contact email.
  • Search by trail name, trail, year, or location.
  • Message a hiker through a contact form — email addresses are never shown publicly.

Share a trail name with another hiker? You're in good company — names get passed around out there like good trail magic, so duplicates are welcome here. The trail, years, and hometown on each entry will help you tell who's who. And if you ever need to fix anything on your own entry, the “Edit my entry” link at the bottom of the page will get you there. Be kind out there.
